# Cambel Hurst | Transportation Planning and Policy

Source for my portfolio website: **https://cambelh07-glitch.github.io**

The site presents my work in transit planning, GIS and R analysis, and transportation policy in Northern Virginia:

- **Northern Virginia Transit Access Analysis.** How much scheduled weekday transit service is within walking distance of where people live across nine Northern Virginia jurisdictions (R, ArcGIS Pro, GTFS, ACS).
- **Data Center Siting and Transmission Infrastructure.** A case-control study of how close data centers sit to high-voltage transmission lines (ArcGIS Pro, statistical testing).
- **Addressing the Supply of Housing in Virginia.** A policy memo to the Virginia General Assembly comparing options to expand housing supply.
- **Résumé.**

The site is built with plain HTML and CSS and published with GitHub Pages.
