Northern Virginia Transit Access Analysis

Independent project | September 2026 | R, ArcGIS Pro, GTFS, ACS

How much scheduled weekday transit service is within walking distance of where people live across nine Northern Virginia jurisdictions? Using schedules from eight transit providers and Census data for 2.56 million residents, I found that service intensity differs 38 to 58 times between inner and outer jurisdictions, while minority residents have equal or greater access than their neighbors in eight of nine jurisdictions. The divide in access is geographic rather than demographic.

Data Center Siting and Transmission Infrastructure

Case-control study | ArcGIS Pro, statistical testing

Compared the distance from 199 data centers to high-voltage transmission lines against a set of control sites. Data centers sat a median of 1,377 feet from transmission lines, compared with 3,101 feet for controls, a difference confirmed with a Mann-Whitney U test (p < 0.001).

Addressing the Supply of Housing in Virginia

Policy memo to the Virginia General Assembly | October 2025

Virginia's active home listings fell by about 54 percent between 2016 and 2025 while prices rose about 76 percent. This memo traces the shortage to local supply constraints, compares a state Builder's Remedy, a Pro-Housing Incentive Program, and the status quo on supply, affordability, and political feasibility, and recommends a Builder's Remedy with a phased implementation plan.
